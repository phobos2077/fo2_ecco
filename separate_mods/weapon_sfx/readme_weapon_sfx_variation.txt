Unlocks some of the originally unused variations of weapon sound effects.

Requires sfall 4.4.5 or higher.

Should be compatible with RPU, UPU, EtTu, as well as vanilla game.


Installation:
- Extract contents into game root folder.
- Verify that you have "scripts" folder inside "<GameRoot>\mods\weapon_sfx_variation\".
- Open your "<GameRoot>\mods\mods_order.txt" and add "weapon_sfx_variation" (without quotes) to the end of the file.
